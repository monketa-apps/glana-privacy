Glana · Privacy policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026.

In short: Glana works only on your phone. It has no internet access, collects no personal data and shares nothing.

What Glana reads. To find large and duplicate files, Glana reads the list of your photos, videos and audio files, and the list of files in the folders you choose. To compare possible duplicates, it also reads their content. This happens only on your phone.

What Glana stores on your phone. The results of the last scan, the fingerprints used to spot duplicates, the total space freed, your settings and the trash. While they are in the trash, files from the folders you chose are copied into the app's private storage; photos, videos and audio files stay in Android's own trash. Uninstalling Glana erases all of this, including those copies.

What Glana sends. Nothing. Glana has no internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere. It uses no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services. A file only reaches another app when you open it yourself with “Open with”.

Permissions. Glana asks for access to your photos, videos and audio files, and to the folders you choose yourself. If you allow it, Glana also uses media management, so Android does not ask for a second confirmation when you delete photos and videos. You can withdraw each permission in Android settings.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version comes with an app update and a new date at the top.
