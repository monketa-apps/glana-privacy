Glana · Privacy policy

Glana helps you free up space on your phone. To do so, it reads the list of your photos, videos and audio files, and the list of the folders you choose. To find duplicates, it also reads the content of the files it compares. All of this happens on your phone.

Glana has no internet access, so it cannot send anything. It collects no personal data, uses no analytics, advertising or crash reporting service, and shares nothing. A file only goes to another app when you open it yourself with “Open with”.

On your phone, Glana keeps the results of the last scan, the fingerprints used to spot duplicates, the total space freed and your settings. It also keeps the trash for the duration you chose: a copy of files from chosen folders, while photos, videos and audio files stay in Android's trash. Uninstalling the app erases all of this, including those copies.

The app asks for access to your photos, videos and audio files, and to the folders you choose yourself. If you allow it, it also uses media management, which avoids Android's second confirmation. You can withdraw each of these permissions in Android settings.
