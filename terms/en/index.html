Glana · Terms of use

Last updated: 17 November 2026.

Glana finds large and duplicate files on your Android device so you can free up space. It is provided as is, for your personal use.

You choose which files to move. Files go to Glana's trash first and stay there for the number of days you set; they are deleted for good only when you empty the trash or that time runs out. Check your selection before you confirm.

Glana is a one-time purchase on Google Play. There is no subscription, no in-app purchase and no ads. Refunds follow the Google Play rules.

Glana has no internet access and sends nothing about you or your files. See the privacy policy for the details.

Glana includes open-source components, listed in the app, that keep their own licences (About › Open source licences).

Nothing in these terms limits the rights that mandatory consumer protection law gives you.
